# Elden Ring Full Walkthrough

A zone-by-zone checklist for the whole base game, from the Chapel of Anticipation to the Elden Throne. It's adapted from the Fextralife Elden Ring wiki walkthrough (https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Walkthrough) and its zone pages.

How this file becomes the website page:

- Each "##" heading is a zone. Zone names are always shown on the page.
- Each "###" heading is one checklist step. The text below it is the step's description.
- A line starting with "Point of no return:" marks the step with "!" and shows that text as the warning.
- Anything above the first "##" heading (like this intro) is not shown on the page.
- Saved progress is a count of completed steps, so adding, removing, or reordering steps shifts every reader's progress. Prefer editing a step's text in place.
- Translations live beside this file as `full-walkthrough.<language>.md` (fr, de, es, pt, it). They must have exactly the same zones, steps, and "Point of no return:" lines in the same order (keep that marker in English in every file); the build fails if they drift. When you change a step here, change it in every translation too.

## 0. Tutorial

### Shape your Tarnished and search the chapel
Pick a class and a Keepsake. In the Chapel of Anticipation, take the Tarnished's Wizened Finger and "The Ring" gesture from the glowing corpse.

### Stand against the Grafted Scion
The Grafted Scion strikes once you cross the wooden bridge toward the great statue. This fight is meant to be lost. A win earns you its gear, and The Four Belfries lead back here much later.

### Gather yourself in the Stranded Graveyard
You rise with 3 Flasks of Crimson Tears and 1 Flask of Cerulean Tears. Speak with the Commoner seated in the chair. The door at the top of the stairs by the golden tree lets you bypass the optional tutorial.

### Defeat the Soldier of Godrick (optional tutorial)
Drop into the pit to enter the Cave of Knowledge and follow its lessons to the golden fog. The Soldier of Godrick is a feeble foe worth 400 runes. Then search the hanging corpse for the "Strength!" gesture, and the body beside the archway for a Finger Severer and 2 Tarnished's Furled Fingers.

### Note the Fringefolk Hero's Grave, then depart
Near the exit, a two-headed imp statue bars the Fringefolk Hero's Grave and asks for two Stonesword Keys. The dungeon beyond is deadly, so return far later. Touch the Site of Grace, ride the lift, and push through the doors to The First Step.

## 1. West Limgrave

### Speak with White Mask Varré
Varré waits by The First Step. Hear him out until he has nothing more to say. His questline offers one road into Mohgwyn Palace later on.

### Buy a Crafting Kit and Torch from Kalé
Kalé trades from inside the Church of Elleh, a short ride north of The First Step. Purchase the Crafting Kit and Torch, examine the Smithing Table for a Smithing Stone Shard, and search the foot of the crucifix outside for a Fringefolk's Rune. The Tree Sentinel roams close by, so keep it away from the church for now.

### Meet Melina at the Gatefront Site of Grace
Harvest Rowa Fruit, Erdleaf Flowers, and animal bones as you head north. Once you've discovered three Sites of Grace, Melina appears at Gatefront. She grants you the power to level up and hands over the Spectral Steed Whistle to call Torrent.

### Meet Renna by night
Pass the time at the Church of Elleh's Site of Grace until night falls. Renna stands on the church's north wall. Tell her you hold the Spectral Steed and she bestows the Spirit Calling Bell and the Lone Wolf Ashes.

### Clear the Gatefront Ruins camp
Lure the soldiers out one by one, since any who spot you will sound the alarm. The carriage chests contain the Lordsworn's Greatsword and a Flail. The map fragment for Map: Limgrave, West sits on the central pillar, and the cellar below holds the Whetstone Knife and Ash of War: Storm Stomp.

### Free Boc from the illusory tree
Ride south from the Agheel Lake North Site of Grace to the grove where sheep graze. Strike the little yellow tree to uncover Boc, then speak with him. Meeting him here is what begins his Coastal Cave quest.

### Clear Limgrave Tunnels
The mine lies southeast of Gatefront Ruins. Carry a Torch. Smithing Stones line its tunnels, and the Stonedigger Troll at its end yields the Roar Medallion.

### Clear Groveside Cave and Coastal Cave
Groveside Cave, west of the Church of Elleh, ends with the Beastman of Farum Azula, who yields the Flamedrake Talisman. Coastal Cave, on the western shore, ends with two Demi-Human Chiefs, who yield the Tailoring Tools and a Sewing Needle. Hand the Sewing Needle to Boc. On the beach, slay the invisible Teardrop Scarab for Ash of War: Stamp (Sweep).

### Clear Stormfoot Catacombs
Find the catacombs north of the Church of Elleh. Imps lie in ambush, and the flamethrower trap can be shut off from behind. Pull the lever beside the reaper statue, then fell the Erdtree Burial Watchdog for the Noble Sorcerer Ashes.

### Free Sorceress Sellen at Waypoint Ruins
Slay the Mad Pumpkin Head in the cellar and open the steel door beyond. Take Sellen as your teacher and her sorceries become yours to buy.

### Decide Patches's fate in Murkwater Cave
Murkwater Cave sits up the ravine from Agheel Lake. Patches yields once he's at half health. Spare and forgive him and he opens a shop that sells Margit's Shackle and Stonesword Keys. Slay him and you claim his Bell Bearing.
Point of no return: Slaying Patches here ends his questline and his shop for the rest of this playthrough. Sparing him preserves both.

### Optional: Fell the Tree Sentinel
When your weapon reaches +1 or +2, face the Tree Sentinel on horseback. Victory earns the Golden Halberd.

### Force your way through Stormgate into Stormhill
Gallop past the soldiers and the Troll at Stormgate, or bring the Troll down first. Claim the Golden Seed from the golden tree at the top of the path, just before the shack, then rest at the Stormhill Shack Site of Grace.

### Speak with Roderika and search Stormhill
Roderika shelters in the shack. Exhaust her dialogue to receive the Spirit Jellyfish Ashes. A Stonesword Key lies close by, and the Godrick Soldier Ashes rest in the cemetery southwest of the shack.

### Meet Knight Bernahl at the Warmaster's Shack
Speak with Bernahl to open his Ashes of War shop. By night, the Bell Bearing Hunter takes his place. To make this mini-boss appear, rest at the Warmaster's Shack Site of Grace and pass time to nightfall, rest again, then enter the shack. Be warned: this fight isn't recommended yet. Bell Bearing Hunters are far tougher than the zones they appear in. If you defeat him, offer his Bone Peddler's Bell Bearing to the Twin Maiden Husks in Roundtable Hold.

### Free Iron Fist Alexander
Northeast of the Warmaster's Shack, kill the octopuses, then strike Alexander with heavy attacks until he bursts free of the earth. He'll point you toward the festival at Redmane Castle.

### Meet the North Limgrave merchant
Beyond the Saintsbridge Site of Grace, seek out the Nomadic Merchant of North Limgrave.

## 2. East Limgrave and Mistwood

### Visit the Third Church of Marika
No foes guard this church. Take the Flask of Wondrous Physick and the Crimson Crystal Tear, and rest at its Site of Grace. Leave the Giant Runebears be for now.

### Search the Minor Erdtree in Mistwood
At the roots of the Minor Erdtree, gather the Map: Limgrave, East, a Greenspill Crystal Tear, and a Spiked Cracked Tear. A Teardrop Scarab nearby yields Ash of War: Ground Slam.

### Uncover the hidden Mistwood Ruins
South of the Third Church, examine the spectral tree and trail the ghostly footsteps to the ruins. A chest in the cellar holds the Axe Talisman. Blaidd keeps watch from the high ruins. Buy the Finger Snap gesture from Kalé and perform it beneath him to begin his quest.

### Descend to the Siofra River
The lift in Mistwood Ruins carries you down to Siofra River. Touch the Site of Grace at the bottom, then ride back up. The foes below are meant for stronger Tarnished.

### Liberate Fort Haight for Kenneth Haight
Kenneth Haight stands on the cliffs north of the Third Church. Agree to aid him. Fort Haight lies down the southeast road. Within, claim the Dectus Medallion (Left), Ash of War: Bloody Slash, and the Golden Seed beside the stairs. Return to Kenneth to receive the Erdsteel Dagger.

### Defeat Bloodhound Knight Darriwil in the Forlorn Hound Evergaol
Darriwil is sealed in the Forlorn Hound Evergaol. If you've met Blaidd at Mistwood Ruins, you can summon him for this fight. Once Darriwil falls, speak with Blaidd at the Evergaol. Tarnished who win this fight before meeting Blaidd need not worry: meet him at Mistwood Ruins first, and he'll then be waiting here.

### Speak with Blaidd twice
The first talk earns a Somber Smithing Stone [2]. On the second, Blaidd sends you to a blacksmith on the Road to the Manor in Liurnia. Tell the smith that "Blaidd sent you" to add the Carian Filigreed Crest to his shop.
Point of no return: If Ranni's quest sends Blaidd to Siofra River before you speak with him here, you lose the Somber Smithing Stone.

### Slay Flying Dragon Agheel
Agheel descends on the Dragon-Burnt Ruins. Face this dragon while riding Torrent. This dragon yields a Dragon Heart, which the Church of Dragon Communion will trade for dragon incantations.

### Take the Homing Instinct painting from the Artist's Shack
The Artist's Shack sits on the high ground southwest of Summonwater Village and northeast of the Murkwater Coast Site of Grace. From the outskirts of Summonwater Village, ride south on Torrent, climb the rocks ahead, and follow the cliff to the shack (a Spiritspring nearby also springs you up). Inside, examine the canvas to take the Homing Instinct painting, and grab the Smithing Stone [1] while you're there. Carry the painting to the small grave on the west side of the Dragon-Burnt Ruins. The spectral painter appears there only while the painting is in your inventory, and the Incantation Scarab waits beside him. This helmet lowers the FP cost of incantations but lowers your damage negation.

### Recover the Royal House Scroll for Sellen
From the Agheel Lake South Site of Grace, ride northeast, cross the path and then ride up a hill on your right to go south into a camp. There is a corpse at the top of the left (east) square building structure that has the Royal House Scroll. Deliver it to Sellen (at Waypoint Ruins Site of Grace) and she'll teach Glintblade Phalanx and Carian Slicer.

## 3. Weeping Peninsula

### Cross the Bridge of Sacrifice
Follow the road southeast from the Agheel Lake South Site of Grace. Soldiers and a ballista hold the bridge. The Bridge of Sacrifice Site of Grace waits on the far side.

### Accept Irina's Letter
Irina sits upon a rock just past the bridge. Having fled Castle Morne, she entrusts you with Irina's Letter for her father, Edgar. So begins their questline.

### Claim the Weeping Peninsula map
The map fragment stands in an open field beside the southbound road.

### Search Oridys's Rise
Oridys's Rise hides a Memory Stone.

### Clear Impaler's Catacombs
Impaler's Catacombs ends with an Erdtree Burial Watchdog and the Demi-Human Ashes.

### Search Callu Baptismal Church
Callu Baptismal Church holds Flame of Frenzy and a Sacred Tear.

### Clear Morne Tunnel
Morne Tunnel houses the Scaly Misbegotten and a cache of Smithing Stones.

### Fell the Erdtree Avatar at the Minor Erdtree
The Avatar yields a Crystal Tear for your Flask of Wondrous Physick.

### Clear Castle Morne
Ride the lift up and cut through the Misbegotten. On the tower rooftop, find Edgar and deliver the letter. He rewards you with a Sacrificial Twig.

### Slay the Leonine Misbegotten
The boss waits past the golden fog near the castle ladder and yields the Grafted Blade Greatsword. Speak with Edgar once it falls.

### Return to Irina
Head back to where Irina sat near the Bridge of Sacrifice. This advances Edgar and Irina's questline. Edgar will later invade you in Liurnia.

## 4. Roundtable Hold

### Answer Melina's summons
Touch a Site of Grace beyond Limgrave and the Weeping Peninsula, or challenge Margit at Castleward Tunnel, then rest. Melina offers to take you to the Roundtable Hold. Afterward, you can travel there from the bottom-left corner of the map.

### Meet those who dwell in the hold
Speak with everyone. Gideon Ofnir presides over the hold, Corhyn sells incantations, and Diallos seeks a servant. Show D a Deathroot and he'll mark Gurranq's location. Fia offers an embrace that leaves you with a debuff.

### Strengthen your arms with Hewg and the Twin Maiden Husks
Smithing Master Hewg upgrades your armaments. Bell Bearings given to the Twin Maiden Husks expand their wares.

### Unseal the Stonesword Key doors
One Stonesword Key opens the first imp statue, behind which lies Crepus's Black-Key Crossbow. Two more open the second, revealing the Assassin's Prayerbook.

### Mind the lower level
Leaping from the balcony leads to Mad Tongue Alberich, a dangerous invader. Defeat him for the Taunter's Tongue, or leave him be for now.

## 5. Stormveil Castle

### Defeat Margit, the Fell Omen
Margit bars the bridge at Castleward Tunnel. Margit's Shackle binds him twice during his first phase. Rest at the Stormveil Main Gate Site of Grace once he falls.

### Follow Gostoc's side path
Gatekeeper Gostoc steers you away from the well-guarded main gate and toward a side entrance. Heed him, as ballistae cover the main gate route.

### Clear the cliffside
Make use of the summoning pool, take the Golden Rune and Smithing Stones, clear out the Warhawk's eyrie, and rest at the Stormveil Cliffside Site of Grace.

### Clear the Wine Cellar and the Banished Knight
Search the Wine Cellar for Hookclaws and throwing items. The first Banished Knight fights in darkness, so bring a Torch. He guards the Rusty Key, which opens the way to the Rampart Tower Site of Grace.

### Cross the rooftops to the armory
Pick off the crossbowmen from range and take a Stonesword Key from a corpse. Use it on the imp statue to enter the armory, where the Misericorde and the Iron Whetblade await.

### Slip past the Grafted Scion
The Scion's chamber holds the Chrysalids' Memento for Roderika and a Lump of Flesh. Sneak past it, or slay it for runes. Keep the Memento for Roderika once the castle is behind you.

### Meet Sorcerer Rogier
Rogier rests in the chapel and sells Ashes of War. Exhaust his dialogue.

### Raid the courtyard storeroom
Exile Soldiers swarm the courtyard, so draw them into the stable. Beneath the courtyard, a Stonesword Key door conceals the Godslayer's Seal and the Godskin Prayerbook.

### Reach the Liftside Chamber and Secluded Cell
Rest at the Liftside Chamber Site of Grace and ride the lift down. Creep up on the Teardrop Scarab below for Ash of War: Stormcaller, then touch the Secluded Cell Site of Grace. The boss door is close at hand.

### Speak with Nepheli Loux
Nepheli waits in a room near the boss door. Exhaust her dialogue so you can summon her against Godrick. A Golden Seed glows on the golden tree near the Troll in this area.

### Slay the Lion Guardian and open the main gate
Fell the Lion Guardian in the main courtyard for a Somber Smithing Stone. Take the Boltdrake Talisman near the Limgrave Tower Bridge Site of Grace, then pull the lever to raise the main gate.

### Defeat Godrick the Grafted
Summon Nepheli at the golden fog. In his second phase, Godrick grafts a dragon's head to his arm and breathes fire. He yields Godrick's Great Rune, the Remembrance of the Grafted, and 15,000 runes. Search the west side of the throne room for a Shabriri Grape.

### Restore Godrick's Great Rune
From the Limgrave Tower Bridge Site of Grace, cross the bridge east to the Divine Tower of Limgrave and activate the sigil at its peak. Equip the Great Rune at any Site of Grace, then use a Rune Arc to awaken its power.

### Send Roderika to the Roundtable Hold
Return to the Stormhill Shack and give Roderika the Chrysalids' Memento. She then moves to the Roundtable Hold. Travel to the hold and speak with her there, then return to the now-empty shack to claim the Golden Seed she left behind.

### Optional: Clear the Fringefolk Hero's Grave
Fast travel to the Stranded Graveyard Site of Grace and spend two Stonesword Keys at the imp statue. Death-blight chariots patrol the trenches, so dash between the alcoves, and two Godrick Knights guard the halls beyond. While traversing the ramps, you will come to a decision to go up or down. Go up the ramp first to fight a knight to get the Banished Knight Oleg Spirit Ash. The Ulcerated Tree Spirit waits at the end and yields a Golden Seed.

### Optional: Defeat the Crucible Knight in the Stormhill Evergaol
The Stormhill Evergaol lies a short ride northwest of the Stormhill Shack Site of Grace. A Crucible Knight is sealed within. He punishes greed, so learn his shield bash and tail sweep, and expect him to take flight in his second phase. He yields the Aspects of the Crucible: Tail incantation. If you have not already done so, look just to the north for what looks like the entrance to a castle; inside is the Arrow's Reach Talisman.

## 6. Liurnia of the Lakes (South)

### Reach the Lake-Facing Cliffs
Exit Stormveil from the rear and rest at the Lake-Facing Cliffs Site of Grace. Boc settles here and offers to alter your armor. If he's absent, rest again. Optional: Make sure you get the first Shabriri Grape before exiting Stormveil Castle and give it to Lightseeker Hyetta near the Lake-Facing Cliffs Site of Grace. Hyetta will then move to the Purified Ruins. This begins the questline to unlock the Frenzied Flame ending. Doing the grape steps does not prevent you from other endings at this time. A warning will appear later in the guide when that happens.

### Visit Thops at the Church of Irith
Thops sells sorceries for a modest offering. The church also holds a Sacred Tear, and an Academy Scroll lies in the graveyard.

### Defeat Adan, Thief of Fire
Malefactor's Evergaol sits on a cliff below the church. Adan yields the Flame of the Fell God incantation.

### Clear Stillwater Cave
Carry a Torch and Neutralizing Boluses. Claim the Sage Set. The Cleanrot Knight at the cave's end yields the Winged Sword Insignia.

### Recover Rya's necklace from Boggart
Rya resides near the telescope on the map. She sends you to Boggart at the Boilprawn Shack, who sells you her necklace for 1,000 runes. You take it back to Rya, who offers you an invitation to Volcano Manor. Then you go back to Boggart and keep talking to him to advance his own questline.

### Explore the lakeshore and Rose Church
Collect the Map: Liurnia East. At the Rose Church, Varré gives you the Festering Bloody Finger.

### Find Patches on Scenic Isle
Patches has moved to the little island of Scenic Isle in the lake. Rest at the Scenic Isle Site of Grace and speak with him. If you spared him in Murkwater Cave, his shop is open here too.

### Find Albus in the Village of the Albinaurics
The Village of the Albinaurics lies in southwest Liurnia. From the Folly on the Lake Site of Grace go directly south, to a large covered area. On the southern wall is a long ramp up to a higher level. Follow that up to find the Village of the Albinaurics Site of Grace. From there, turn around and stay against the right (south) wall. Fight the assassin. Then keep going to see Albus disguised as a pot. Strike the pot to reveal him, then speak with him to receive the Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right). Warning: Picking up either half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion triggers an invasion by Ensha of the Royal Remains the next time you fast-travel to the Roundtable Hold. Ensha attacks as soon as you arrive, so be ready to fight. Defeat Ensha for the Royal Remains set and the Clinging Bone. Another caution is that if you are rune-arched and Godrick's Great Rune is active, all of your stats are raised by 5. When you return to Roundtable Hold and are invaded, those stats will be temporarily removed. If your weapons and gear are maxed with Godrick's raised stats, losing those stats suddenly may put you in a situation where your weapon is unusable (not enough strength or dexterity) and you also may switch from Medium to Heavy load. This could put you in an unexpected, unfair disadvantage. And because Ensha attacks aggressively right away, you will die before you figure out what happened. Bottom line: before returning to Roundtable Hold, make sure that you are ready even if all your stats are lowered by 5.

### Clear the Lakeside Crystal Cave
Once you enter a large open cavern area, stay left and go up a rocky path. This will lead to a cave with a miniboss that has the Spear Talisman. Then make your way down to the bottom of the cavern to slay the Bloodhound Knight for the Cerulean Amber Medallion. After defeating it, go out of the back of the cave to find the Slumbering Wolf's Shack Site of Grace. Talk to Latenna to get her Spirit Ash.

### Obtain the Academy Glintstone Key
The key rests on a corpse upon the small island west of the academy, watched over by Glintstone Dragon Smarag. You can sneak behind this dragon and snatch it. Slaying this dragon later yields a Dragon Heart.

## 7. Raya Lucaria Library

### Enter the academy
Claim the Golden Seed in Academy Gate Town and slay the Teardrop Scarab for Ash of War: Charge Forth. Use the Academy Glintstone Key at the South Raya Lucaria Gate, then rest at the Main Academy Gate Site of Grace.

### Claim the bridge's Golden Seed and invade to help Yura
Turn to the north and DO NOT examine the seal, so you simply pass through it. There is a summoning pool to the left. Keep going north down the bridge, ignoring the red summon sign for now. Mount Torrent and jump the gap. At the end of the bridge, take the Golden Seed from the golden tree. Turn back and touch the red summon sign to invade and help Yura, then rest again at the Main Academy Gate Site of Grace.

### Buy the Fanged Imp Ashes and snipe the gate assassin
Go east and DO NOT examine the seal, so you simply pass through it. Keep going down the bridge until you find a merchant. Buy the Fanged Imp Ashes, as you will need them later. Past the merchant you will see the South Gate. Use lock-on to spot the assassin hiding high up on the gate, then snipe him to start the fight. Afterward, fast travel back to the Main Academy Gate Site of Grace, rest, then go southwest to the lift.

### Clear the Church of the Cuckoo and its graveyard
After clearing the enemies and looting the church, exit to the left (east) and make a quick left to rest at the Church of the Cuckoo Site of Grace. Leave the church and in the graveyard go down and to the left, before the rope bridge there will be a drop down path. Follow that around until you get the Marionette Soldier Ashes. After dropping from there you will be on the other side of the rope bridge. After that, if you drop down to the right you will get the Carian Knight armor set.

### Defeat the Alabaster Lord at the monolith
When you see the continuous rising lift, take it before going forward so you can jump off on the platform above, continue into the building and rest at the Schoolhouse Classroom Site of Grace. Head back out from where you came, run up the ramp and take the other side of the lift back down. Jump off the lift on the other side. You will be lower from where you started in the area. Go left to clear and grab the items there and go back to where you can take the lift back up. Take it and jump off at the bridge and make a right to head to the monolith that will spawn in the Alabaster Lord who will give you the Gravity Well sorcery.

### Optional: Grab the Longtail Cat Talisman
Exit the Schoolhouse Classroom Site of Grace the way you came in and take the lift all the way to the bottom. If you let the Abductor Virgin capture you, as Patches told you to do, you will die and be teleported to the back of Volcano Manor in Mt. Gelmir, which will be too hard if this is your first playthrough. Avoid it, grab the Longtail Cat Talisman, and run up the ramp to leave the area.

### Search the Schoolhouse Classroom
From the Schoolhouse Classroom Site of Grace exit to the south and make a left. In the next room a hidden door/illusory wall will lead to the Conspectus Scroll, the Glintstone Craftsman's Cookbook [5], and the Olivinus Glintstone Crown. Rest at the same grace again and it is recommended to use the summoning pool to summon a real furled finger to help. The next area has many enemies with ranged attacks that can be frustrating to solo.

### Slay the Red Wolf of Radagon
Go to the top of the stairs. To the left before the boss hallway is an illusory wall on a book shelf. Hit that and follow that path all the way to get back to where you came from then go back to the top of the stairs and enter boss fight. After defeating the wolf, reveals the Debate Parlor Site of Grace.

### Get Radagon Icon Talisman
The Debate Parlor Site of Grace is a hub that you take multiple paths from. The first path is exit to the north and grab the summoning pool before the exit. Make an immediate right turn and right again around the building to find a ladder. Take the ladder, clear the upper area, grab the Radagon Icon talisman from the chest and then drop down to the right. Rest at the Debate Parlor Site of Grace.

### Get the Imbued Sword Key
Exit the Debate Parlor Site of Grace to the north and go left. Go up the stairs and then jump down to the left. Kill the sorcerers then turn around and go down the stairs to open a shortcut. Go back up to the top of the stairs and jump off the balcony to the left. Kill the marionette soldiers and go up the ladder. Go across the walkway. Drop down, go right and grab the Imbued Sword Key. Jump back across, jump down to the building below. When on the rooftops, do not drop down. Instead go through a middle window to traverse ceiling beams of the church. Grab the item then look for the two chandeliers below where one of them has an item. Drop down to that chandelier and grab the Academy Glintstone Key. Drop down again then go outside to grab the Twinsage Glintstone Crown that gives +5 to Intelligence.

### Defeat Moongrum, Carian Knight
You will notice a teleporter on the right side of the hallway before Moongrum. Ignore it or else it will take you to the Church of Vows in Liurnia of the Lakes (East). Moongrum guards the lift to the boss and drinks from his flasks to heal, so give him no respite. He yields the Carian Knight's Shield.

### Defeat Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon
In the first phase, strike the glowing student to shatter Rennala's barrier. The second phase is a battle of sorceries. She yields the Great Rune of the Unborn, which counts toward entering Leyndell, and the Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen. From then on she offers Rebirth in exchange for Larval Tears.

### Obtain your 3rd talisman slot
Now that you have obtained your 2nd Great Rune, return to Roundtable Hold and speak with Finger Reader Enia to obtain your 3rd talisman slot. Change your Equipment to add a 3rd talisman, then speak with Sir Gideon Ofnir in the library.

### Decide what to do with Thops and the spare key
Thops longs for the second Academy Glintstone Key. Buy anything you want from him before you hand it over.
Point of no return: Giving Thops the key leads to his death. You'll find his body outside the Schoolhouse Classroom with his Bell Bearing and Thops's Barrier, but you'll never speak with him again. Exit the Schoolhouse Classroom towards the lift. Make a right U-turn and there will be Thops's body.

## 8. Liurnia of the Lakes (East)

### Search the Purified Ruins
As you move up the eastern side of Liurnia, you will find the Liurnia Highway South Site of Grace. To the north west of that will be The Purified Ruins. The Purified Ruins hold the Two Fingers Heirloom and another grape for Hyetta. Ignore the waygate to the academy's entrance. Hyetta can now be found on the west side of the westernmost structure. After getting the 2nd grape from the underground ruins, give it to Hyetta. She will then move to standing against the rock face just north of the Gate Town Bridge Site of Grace.

### Clear Cliffbottom Catacombs
From the Liurnia Highway North Site of Grace, go northeast to the Guide and Gatekeeper statue. Interact with it and it reveals the way south to the Cliffbottom Catacombs. Beware the imps and floor traps, and pull the lever deep within. The staff-bearing Erdtree Burial Watchdog yields the Kaiden Sellsword Ashes.

### Find Gate Town Bridge Site of Grace
From the Liurnia Highway North Site of Grace, go north and slightly left through the tunnel and follow the path until you get to the Gate Town Bridge Site of Grace. Optional: if you're doing Hyetta's questline, she should be just north of this grace waiting for the 3rd Shabriri Grape. You will not get the 3rd grape until you explore Liurnia of the Lakes (West). But if you want to get it now, go directly west from Temple Quarter to find the Revenger's Shack. Rest, pass time to nightfall, then rest again to trigger an invasion. Defeat the invader Edgar the Revenger to obtain Hyetta's 3rd Shabriri Grape. Return to the Gate Town Bridge Site of Grace to give it to Hyetta. She will then move to the Church of Inhibition in the far northeastern hills of Liurnia, past the Frenzied Flame Village.

### Claim the Carian Glintblade Staff from the Highway Lookout Tower
The lone tower overlooks the main road on the eastern landmass. Ride north along the road and veer left by the cliffs. A Twinsage Sorcerer on the top floor snipes with glintstone, and spectral Noble Sorcerers and Godrick soldiers fill the hill below, so ride past them and go straight inside. Climb the first ladder to a chest with the Glintstone Craftsman's Cookbook [3], then two more ladders to the top. Kill the sorcerer and loot the corpse beside him for the Carian Glintblade Staff.

### Slay the Tibia Mariner
It drifts east of the Gate Town Bridge Site of Grace and yields a Deathroot and the Skeletal Bandit Ashes. Show the Deathroot to D or carry it to Gurranq in Caelid.

### Visit the Church of Vows
Miriel, Pastor of Vows, teaches sorceries and incantations. The church also holds the Golden Tailoring Tools and the Gold Sewing Needle for Boc; open the chest near the statue to get them. Return to the Lake-Facing Cliffs Site of Grace to give Boc the Gold Sewing Needle and Golden Tailoring Tools.

### Bring down a Walking Mausoleum
Shatter the skull deposits on its body until it collapses, then step inside to duplicate a Remembrance. Each mausoleum works only once, so choose a Remembrance you'll truly use.

### Fell the Erdtree Avatar at the northeast Minor Erdtree
It yields the Lightning, Magic, and Holy Shrouding Cracked Tears.

### Clear Raya Lucaria Crystal Tunnel

### Explore the far north

### Clear Black Knife Catacombs
From the Ruined Labyrinth Site of Grace, head northeast past the Minor Erdtree to the catacombs; a Mausoleum Knight guards the entrance. On the stairs below the Site of Grace, run past the first two black flame respawning skeletons (you can tell them apart from ordinary skeletons by the black flame on their torsos) and kill the Necromancer around the corner. Until the necromancer dies, those black flame skeletons will keep getting back up endlessly. Inside, the locked doors flanked by statues open only from a lever deep in the dungeon. The first left leads to an imp-statue room: use a Stonesword Key to find Rosus' Axe on a body against a tombstone. In the guillotine room, there are more black flame skeleton archers, so there is no point killing them. Instead, ride the 2nd falling blade up to a ledge and jump to it where you will find the 2nd necromancer. Next room, same deal, ignore the black flame skeletons and kill the 3rd necromancer around the corner. Strike the wall at the end to reveal the fog gate to the Black Knife Assassin, who yields the Assassin's Cerulean Dagger and the Black Knifeprint for Rogier's quest. D, Hunter of the Dead, may offer a summon sign for this fight, depending on his quest. The Assassin is optional for the rest of the dungeon. After defeating the Assassin, do not use the "Return to Entrance" teleport. Instead, leave the room and drop down the hole along the left wall. Return through those doors to fight the Cemetery Shade and its skeletons; block its binding green bolts with the pillars. It yields the Twinsage Sorcerer Ashes, and a chest in its room holds Deathroot.

## 25. Leyndell, Capital of Ash

### Awaken in the Capital of Ash
You awaken at the Leyndell, Capital of Ash Site of Grace. Drop into the sewer hole to the southwest for the Crimson Amber Medallion +2.

### Conclude Goldmask and Corhyn's questline
If you completed Goldmask's questline, he stands at the base of the cliff by the giant spear with the Mending Rune of Perfect Order.

### Find the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone
Pass beneath the dragon's wing and climb the ladder. Follow the path past the golden tree to the Fortified Manor doors. Beware the Valiant Gargoyle.

### Defeat Sir Gideon Ofnir
Climb the curving staircase and ride the lift into the Erdtree Sanctuary. Gideon yields the All-Knowing Set and the Scepter of the All-Knowing.

### Defeat Godfrey, First Elden Lord
Summon Nepheli if you made her ruler of Limgrave, or Shabriri if you met the Three Fingers. At half health, Godfrey casts off his name and fights as Hoarah Loux. Defeat him for the Remembrance of Hoarah Loux.

### Defeat Radagon and the Elden Beast
Touch the golden light atop the stairs past the Elden Throne Site of Grace. Radagon of the Golden Order fights first, then the Elden Beast. Together they yield the Elden Remembrance.

### Choose your ending
Touch Marika's remains for the Elden Lord ending, or use a Mending Rune for another fate. If Ranni's quest is complete, her summon sign brings the Age of the Stars ending. If you bear the Frenzied Flame, only Lord of Frenzied Flame remains. After the credits, choose New Game+ or return to the Roundtable Hold.
